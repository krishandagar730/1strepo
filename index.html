About the College

Built on discipline, kept current by industry.

Founder's Block · Main Campus, Est. 1994

Dagar College of Information Technology was founded in 1994 with a single purpose: to train engineers who are as strong in fundamentals as they are fluent in current technology. What began as a two-department institution now runs eight undergraduate and postgraduate programs across computing, data and information systems.

Our classrooms run on a simple principle — theory is taught alongside its application. Every core course carries a lab component, every final year carries an industry project, and every department maintains direct ties with recruiting partners who shape our curriculum reviews each year.

The result is a college that measures itself less by rankings and more by where its graduates are five years after they leave.

Dr. R. Dagar Founder & Chairperson, Governing Council
